1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal Information You Disclose to Us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you request information, use our services, or contact us.

We do not process sensitive personal information.

Information Automatically Collected

We may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and website usage data.

We also collect information through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to provide, improve, and maintain our services, communicate with you, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On?

Depending on your location, we may process your information based on:

  • Your consent
  • Legal obligations
  • Protection of vital interests
  • Compliance with applicable laws

Canada

We process your personal information with your consent, whether express or implied where permitted by law. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

In certain circumstances, applicable laws may allow us to collect, use, or disclose personal information without consent, including for:

  • Fraud prevention and investigations
  • Legal, regulatory, or court requirements
  • Business transactions permitted by law
  • Protecting individuals' health, safety, or vital interests
  • Insurance claim assessments
  • Publicly available information authorized by law
  • Journalistic, artistic, literary, research, or statistical purposes where permitted

EU and UK

If you are located in the EU or UK, we process personal information in accordance with the GDPR and UK GDPR. We may process your information based on your consent, to comply with legal obligations, or to protect an individual's vital interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

We may share personal information with trusted service providers, including Google Maps services when used on our website, or as required during business transfers such as mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve our website, analyze traffic, and remember user preferences. We may use third-party analytics services, including Google Analytics. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Notice or as required by law.

When personal information is no longer needed, we will securely delete, anonymize, or store it until deletion becomes possible.

7. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

We do not knowingly collect, use, or market personal information from individuals under 18 years of age (or the minimum age required by applicable law).

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a minor without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information promptly.

If you believe we may have collected information from a minor, please contact us at asyafrontend@gmail.com.

8. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information.

You may also have the right to withdraw your consent to the processing of your personal information where consent is the legal basis for processing.

To exercise your privacy rights or submit a complaint, please contact us at asyafrontend@gmail.com.

You can also manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

9. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Some browsers offer a Do-Not-Track (DNT) feature. Because there is currently no universal standard for recognizing DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.

Where supported, we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals and apply applicable privacy preferences automatically.
